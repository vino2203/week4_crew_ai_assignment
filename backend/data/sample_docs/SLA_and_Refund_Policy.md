# SLA and Refund Policy

## Page 1

### Service Level Agreement (SLA)

We guarantee 99.9% monthly uptime for the CloudSync platform. Uptime is
measured as the percentage of time the core sync API is reachable and
functioning, excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least 48
hours in advance via the status page.

If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, affected customers on Business and
Enterprise plans automatically receive service credits per the table below:

- 99.0% - 99.9% uptime: 10% credit on that month's invoice
- 95.0% - 99.0% uptime: 25% credit on that month's invoice
- Below 95.0% uptime: 50% credit on that month's invoice

## Page 2

### Requesting a Refund

Refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of the charge via Support >
Billing Issue. Eligible reasons include: accidental duplicate charges, service
outages exceeding SLA thresholds not automatically credited, and cancellations
within the first 14 days of a new subscription ("cooling-off period").

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-10 business
days of approval. Enterprise customers with an annual contract should contact
their account manager instead of the standard support queue.

## Page 3

### Cancellations and Downgrades

Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from Admin Console > Billing > Cancel
Plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; no
partial-month refund is issued for cancellations outside the 14-day
cooling-off period.

Downgrading from Enterprise to Business or Business to Free requires that
current usage (users, storage) fit within the lower tier's limits first. The
admin portal will block a downgrade and list the specific limits exceeded if
this is not the case.
